The launch of Phase 2 for "Neverness to Everness" marks a significant moment for Hotta Studio, especially as it navigates the turbulent waters of an ongoing AI controversy. Despite the challenges, the game has introduced exciting new content, including a limited S-class character, Hotori, and a fresh event lineup that runs until June 3. This resilience showcases the studio's commitment to its player base and the game's evolving narrative.

Hotori is featured on the Misty Tipsy Style Limited Board, which utilizes a unique gacha system that encourages player engagement through its board game mechanics. With player-friendly features like accumulated pity rates, the game is designed to keep players invested and excited about their chances to pull new characters. As the community rallies around these updates, it will be interesting to see how the AI controversy continues to impact player sentiment and the studio's reputation moving forward.

Looking ahead, Hotta Studio has teased two new characters, Lacrimosa and Chaos, hinting at a bright future for the game. Will these developments help the studio regain trust, or will the shadow of the AI controversy linger? Only time will tell.
