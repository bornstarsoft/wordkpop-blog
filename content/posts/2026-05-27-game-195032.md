Escape Simulator, the popular co-op puzzle game that has captivated millions, has officially made its debut on XBOX Game Pass. With over 3.25 million units sold on Steam, this first-person puzzle experience invites players to immerse themselves in a world of intricate challenges, whether solo or with friends in online co-op mode. The game features over 30 original escape rooms, ranging from ancient tombs to sci-fi labs, ensuring that players have a plethora of engaging content to explore.

What sets Escape Simulator apart is not just its captivating gameplay but also its community-driven approach. Players can create their own escape rooms using the built-in editor, share them with friends, and even challenge the wider community. This cross-platform multiplayer support enhances the experience, allowing players on different systems to collaborate and solve puzzles together. As the gaming landscape continues to evolve, Escape Simulator's launch on XBOX Game Pass marks a significant step in making collaborative puzzle-solving accessible to a broader audience.
